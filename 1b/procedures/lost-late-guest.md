## Late at the dock

1. Check-in closes 10 minutes before departure. A guest who arrives after that waits with the dock coordinator. [POLICY]
2. Call the dock lead. The dock lead decides whether a later departure has space and returns in time for the guest's ship. [POLICY]
3. Do not hold the shuttle for a late guest unless the dock lead says so. [POLICY]

## Missing at the glacier or another stop

4. If the headcount is short, the bus stays parked. The driver stays with the bus and the guests. [POLICY]
5. The guide checks the restrooms, the visitor center and the trail start for no more than 10 minutes. [POLICY]
6. After 10 minutes, the guide calls the dock lead. The dock lead decides when the bus leaves and which staff member stays behind. [POLICY]
7. Never leave a guest behind without a staff member and the dock lead's decision. [POLICY] [SAFETY: verify with a qualified person]
8. If a guest may be hurt or in danger, call 911 first, then the dock lead. [SAFETY: verify with a qualified person]

## Cruise guests and their ship

9. If a cruise guest may miss their ship's all-aboard time, the dock lead calls the cruise line's port agent. Staff do not promise that a ship will wait. [POLICY]

## Children

10. A child found alone stays with a staff member in a public, visible spot. The dock lead contacts the parents through reservations. [SAFETY: verify with a qualified person]

## Record

11. Write down what happened: time, departure, where the guest was last seen, what was done. Use the booking reference, not the guest's name. [POLICY]
