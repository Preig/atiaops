## Before the first departure

1. Set up the check-in table at the Channel Light flag on the downtown cruise dock by 7:30. [POLICY]
2. Print the manifest for each departure. It lists booking references, party size, ship and all-aboard time. It lists no guest names or contact details. [POLICY]
3. Check today's port schedule. Write each ship's all-aboard time next to its bookings on the manifest. [POLICY]

## Check guests in

4. Check-in opens 30 minutes before each departure and closes 10 minutes before it. [POLICY]
5. Ask for the booking reference or the cruise line's shore-excursion ticket. Do not ask for ID or payment details. [POLICY]
6. Count the party and match it to the manifest. The minimum age is 3. Children 3 to 12 ride on a child ticket. [POLICY]
7. Give each guest a wristband in the color of their departure: 8:00 blue, 10:30 green, 13:00 orange, 15:30 white. [POLICY]
8. Never board more than 30 guests on one departure. The vessel's certificate of inspection sets this limit. [LAW: vessel certificate of inspection; check your own vessel's certificate]

## Cruise guests and the all-aboard time

9. Planned return to the downtown dock: 8:00 departure at 12:30, 10:30 at 15:00, 13:00 at 17:30, 15:30 at 20:00. [POLICY]
10. Board a cruise guest only on a departure planned to return at least 60 minutes before their ship's all-aboard time. If none fits, call the dock lead. [POLICY]
11. Tell every cruise guest the planned return time and their ship's all-aboard time, out loud, at check-in. [POLICY]

## Money and changes

12. Dock coordinators do not take payments, give refunds or change bookings. Send the guest to reservations. [POLICY]
13. Guests who booked through a cruise line follow the cruise line's terms. Send their refund questions to the cruise line's shore-excursion desk. [POLICY]

## Hand-off

15. When the shuttle leaves, radio the captain at Auke Bay with the headcount for that departure. [POLICY]
