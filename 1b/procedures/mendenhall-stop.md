## The allocation count

1. Our fictional commercial-use allocation is 100 guest visits per day at the glacier. [PERMIT]
2. Every guest who gets off the bus at the glacier counts as one guest visit, children included. Staff and drivers do not count. [PERMIT]
3. Before guests get off the bus, the guide writes the departure's count on the allocation log. [PERMIT]
4. If a party would take the day's log over 100, call the dock lead before anyone gets off the bus. Do not split a party on your own. [POLICY]
5. Never change a count on the allocation log after it is written. If a count is wrong, add a dated note and tell the dock lead. [PERMIT]

## Time on site

6. The stop lasts 50 minutes, from the bus doors opening to the bus doors closing. [POLICY]
7. Tell guests the bus departure time twice: at the bus door and at the visitor center. [POLICY]
8. Do not extend the stop without the dock lead's OK. Cruise guests have an all-aboard time to make. [POLICY]
9. Guests stay on marked trails and behind barriers. [POLICY]
10. If a bear is on or near the trail, keep the group together, walk guests calmly back to the bus and follow any closure the site staff post. [SAFETY: verify with a qualified person]

## Headcount back on the bus

11. Count heads at the bus door. Count again once everyone is seated. Both counts match the number on the allocation log. [POLICY]
12. If the count is short, the bus stays parked. Follow lost-late-guest.md. [POLICY]
