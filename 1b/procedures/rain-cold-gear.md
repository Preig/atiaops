## What we carry

1. Each vessel carries 30 rain ponchos and 10 fleece blankets on every departure. [POLICY]
2. The shuttle carries a box of 30 ponchos for the glacier stop. [POLICY]

## What we tell guests

3. At check-in, tell guests: the trip runs in rain. Bring a rain jacket, warm layers, a hat and gloves. [POLICY]
4. Ponchos are free. Blankets are loaned on board and collected before guests leave the vessel. [POLICY]
5. We do not sell or rent jackets, boots or other gear. [POLICY]

## Cold or unwell guests

6. A guest who is shivering, or a child who is cold and quiet: move them into the heated cabin, give a blanket and tell the captain. [SAFETY: verify with a qualified person]
7. Staff do not give medicine or medical advice. For any medical concern, tell the captain at once. [POLICY] [SAFETY: verify with a qualified person]

## Weather

8. Rain alone does not cancel a trip. A trip cancels for a marine weather warning or a vessel safety call by the captain. [POLICY]
9. Staff do not predict the weather or the go/no-go decision. The captain makes every go/no-go call. [POLICY] [SAFETY: verify with a qualified person]

## After each departure

10. Dry the blankets in the dock office dryer and restock the ponchos to 30. [POLICY]
