# ATIA Operations Kit

Nine practical AI tools for three Alaska tourism operations pains: staff turnover, transit bottlenecks and capacity caps. Built for the ATIA 2026 Annual Convention session "Small Business Focus: Leveraging AI Solutions" (Fairbanks, October 2026).

Open the kit: https://preig.github.io/atiaops/

- Start with the Season Leak Audit (`/leak/`): a dollar range for each pain, from last season's numbers.
- Each pain has three rungs: basic (free tier, an afternoon), intermediate (paid tier or no-code), advanced (developer or analyst).
- Every page works on a phone and sends nothing anywhere. Examples use a fictional teaching business, Channel Light Excursions, Juneau, whose data is generated by `data/make_data.py`.
- AI drafts. A person approves. People decisions, safety calls and prices stay with a person.

Effort, cost and time figures are estimates. Not legal advice. Facts checked 11 October 2026.
