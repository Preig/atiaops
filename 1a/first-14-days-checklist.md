# First 14 days: checklist for every new seasonal hire

From the ATIA Operations Kit, tool 1.a Rehire before you recruit. Written for Channel Light Excursions, Juneau, a **fictional teaching business**. Adapt every line to your operation; the owner approves the final version.

Each item carries a label:

- **Legal mandate**: a law applies. The citation is given; confirm the current text.
- **Contractual**: set by an agreement (sponsor, permit, contract). Check yours.
- **Policy choice**: your decision; you could decide it differently.
- **Recommendation**: my advice, with the reason.

Why it matters: in the fictional 2026 season, 4 of 20 new hires left in their first 30 days, and each was replaced. In the fictional exit notes, 3 of 30 said "Nobody told me the ship-late procedure until it happened," and 1 said "I learned the dock routine from whoever was free that day."

## Before day 1

- [ ] Housing confirmed in writing: address, cost, move-in date, what to bring. **Recommendation**: housing was the top theme in the fictional exit notes.
- [ ] First two weeks of schedule sent, with days off marked. **Policy choice**
- [ ] Pay rate, pay dates and how overtime is paid, in writing. Alaska overtime law applies: AS 23.10.060 (daily and weekly overtime, with exemptions in AS 23.10.055). Confirm with Alaska DOLWD Wage and Hour. **Legal mandate**
- [ ] One named buddy for the first 14 days. **Recommendation**: questions go to one person instead of whoever is free.
- [ ] J-1 or H-2B staff: arrival, housing and pay terms match the sponsor agreement. Check your sponsor agreement and current DOL and State Department rules. **Contractual**
- [ ] New-hire paperwork ready from your payroll provider's checklist. **Recommendation** One deadline to know: Form I-9, employment eligibility verification. The employee completes Section 1 by the first day of work; the employer completes Section 2 within three business days of the first day (8 CFR 274a.2; confirm current text at uscis.gov). **Legal mandate**

## Day 1: welcome

- [ ] Walk-through: dock, boat, office, housing, and where the written procedures live.
- [ ] Safety briefing for the role, given by a qualified person (captain or lead). Safety steps: verify with a qualified person. **Recommendation**
- [ ] Wildlife rules. Humpback whales in Alaska: approaching within 100 yards is prohibited (NOAA Fisheries, 50 CFR 216.18). Confirm current text. **Legal mandate**
- [ ] The ship-late procedure, read together. **Recommendation**
- [ ] Who to ask: the buddy, the lead on shift, the owner.

## Days 2 to 3: shadow

- [ ] Shadow the buddy on full departures. The buddy explains why, not only what.
- [ ] **Day 3 check-in**, 10 minutes, owner or lead: "What is unclear? What is harder than you expected? Is housing working?" Write down one answer and one action. **Recommendation**

## Days 4 to 7: co-lead

- [ ] Routine tasks with the buddy watching (for example: dock check-in, boarding count, safety briefing, by role).
- [ ] One drill of the ship-late and lost-guest procedures.
- [ ] **Day 7 check-in**: schedule for week 3 confirmed; days off taken. **Recommendation**

## Days 8 to 14: solo with a check

- [ ] Routine tasks solo; the buddy checks at the end of each day.
- [ ] Sign-off per task by the lead: task, date, initials. Safety-related tasks are signed off by a qualified person. **Policy choice** (who may sign off)
- [ ] **Day 14 check-in**: what still needs practice; the next check-in date.

## Day 30

- [ ] Last check-in of the early-quit window. Count Number 2: new hires who left in their first 30 days ÷ new hires who started.

## Rules for using this checklist

- Check-ins are about the job, the schedule and housing. They are not performance scores, and no tool ranks or flags individual people. **Policy choice**
- Federal anti-discrimination law (Title VII, ADA, ADEA) applies to employment decisions, whatever tool helps make them. Confirm current text with a qualified person. **Legal mandate**
- If you use AI to adapt this checklist, ask it to mark the result DRAFT - PENDING OWNER REVIEW and to leave [FILL] where it lacks a fact. A person approves the final version. **Recommendation**
